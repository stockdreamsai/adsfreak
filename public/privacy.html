Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what Ali G Marketing LLC ("we", "us") collects when you use Social Ads Freak (the "Service") and how we use it.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: your name and email address, provided directly or via Google sign-in.
  • Purchase data: order details from our payment processor, JVZoo (we do not receive or store your full card number).
  • Content you upload: product images, descriptions, scripts, and — if you choose to create a custom avatar — a photo of yourself.
  • Generated content: the videos, images, and audio the Service creates for you.
  • Usage data: token balances and generation history, plus technical logs needed to run and secure the Service.

2. How we use it

  • To provide the Service and generate your content.
  • To account for token usage and prevent abuse of the Service.
  • To send transactional email such as welcome and password-reset messages.
  • To provide customer support.

Your uploaded photos and product content are used to generate the content you request and to operate the Service — we do not sell your personal information.

3. AI processing and service providers

Generation requests are processed by third-party AI and infrastructure providers acting on our behalf, including Google Cloud (AI models, authentication, and storage), KIE (AI video generation), ElevenLabs (voice), Supabase (database hosting), Amazon Web Services (content delivery), Resend (transactional email), and JVZoo (payments). Each receives only what is needed to perform its function.

4. Cookies

The app uses essential cookies to keep you signed in. The marketing site does not set advertising cookies of its own.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep your account data, uploads, and generated content while your account is active. You can delete projects and videos inside the app; you may also request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us. Purchase records are retained as required for tax and accounting purposes.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal information. To exercise them, email us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children

The Service is a business tool for adults. It is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; material changes will be posted here with an updated date.
