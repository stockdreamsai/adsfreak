Social Ads Freak - sales page
==============================

HOW TO VIEW
Unzip, then open new2.html in a browser. Keep the folder structure as it
is: the page loads new2.css, images/, videos/, captions/ and brand/ by
relative path.

An internet connection is needed. Fonts come from Google Fonts, and the
avatar photos and sample ad videos stream from a CDN.

The footer links open terms.html, privacy.html and disclaimer.html, which
are included.

KNOWN WHEN OPENED FROM DISK
Subtitle tracks on the sample videos do not load from a local file, because
browsers block them. They work once the page is on a web server.
